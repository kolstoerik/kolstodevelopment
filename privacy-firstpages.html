First Pages — Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Effective Date: October 30, 2026 • Operated by Kolstø Development (Org. no. 938 611 130).

1. Our Core Privacy Commitment

Kolstø Development ("we", "us", "our") is the developer and operator of the First Pages mobile application. We recognize that early childhood memories, photographs, and family notes represent deeply private personal data. First Pages is fundamentally built on the principle that parents and legal guardians retain 100% ownership of all their data.

We do not sell, rent, license, or monetize your personal information or media. We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, data brokers, or behavioral tracking networks.

● 2. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

First Pages is an organizational parenting application directed strictly to parents, legal guardians, and adult caregivers (aged 18 and older). The application is neither designed for nor targeted at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 within the European Economic Area).

We never knowingly solicit or collect personal information directly from children. Any information concerning a child—such as a first name, birth date, milestone entries, and photographs—is entered exclusively by the child's parent or verified legal guardian under affirmative parental consent.

3. Information We Collect and Process

  • Account Identifiers: Your email address, display name, and authentication credentials (via Email/Password, Magic Link, Sign in with Apple, or Google Sign-In) required to authenticate your account and sync your books across authorized devices.
  • User-Generated Media & Journal Data: Photographs, milestone dates, journal notes, custom cover texts, and your child's name and birth date, stored exclusively to render and export your memory book. When selecting a photo, the app reads the capture date (EXIF DateTime) locally on your device to suggest a milestone date; GPS location metadata is never extracted or stored on our servers.
  • Co-Parent Collaboration: Temporary 7-day invite codes and basic member identifiers (display name and email) when you voluntarily invite a partner or family member to co-edit a book.
  • Purchase Verification Records: When you unlock a book via Apple App Store or Google Play, we validate the store transaction token and store an encrypted receipt identifier to maintain your book's unlock status. We never receive or process your credit card or payment details.

4. Data Storage, Security & Sub-Processors

All network transit between the mobile application and our infrastructure is enforced via encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. Uploaded photographs are stored in private, access-controlled cloud buckets accessible only to authorized members of your book via time-limited signed URLs:

  • Supabase Inc. (PostgreSQL Database, Authentication & Object Storage): Provides isolated relational database storage, row-level security (RLS), and encrypted media storage under GDPR-compliant Data Processing Agreements (DPA).
  • Apple Inc. & Google LLC: Provide optional OAuth sign-in (Sign in with Apple / Google Sign-In) and native In-App Purchase billing verification.

5. Your Legal Rights & Complete Account Deletion

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable privacy laws, you hold the right to access, rectify, export, or permanently erase your personal data ("Right to be Forgotten").

How to permanently delete your account in the app: Open the First Pages mobile application, navigate to the Profile tab → scroll down to Settings → tap Delete Account, and type DELETE to confirm.

What happens when you delete your account: Executing this action immediately and irreversibly deletes your user account, authentication record, and all solely-owned books, milestone notes, and photographs from our database and cloud storage buckets. If you are a member of a shared book with a co-parent, your membership is removed and book ownership is transferred to the remaining co-parent so their shared family keepsake is not lost. Anonymized store transaction IDs are unlinked from your user ID.

Request deletion via web/email: If you have uninstalled the app and wish to request account or data deletion without logging in, email contact@kolstodevelopment.com from your registered email address.

6. Terms of Service

By creating an account or utilizing First Pages, you agree to these operating terms:

  • Intellectual Property: You retain complete, unrestricted copyright over all images and reflections you upload. You warrant that you hold legitimate custody or parental consent to upload the media.
  • One-Time Book Unlock & Finalization: Creating a draft book is free (up to 2 active unpaid drafts per account). Unlocking a book for co-parent sharing and print-ready PDF export is a one-time in-app purchase per book processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. Finalizing or exporting a book locks the purchase credit to that book and activates a 48-hour proofing window before memories are permanently archived.
  • Acceptable Conduct: You agree not to upload abusive, unlawful, or infringing content. Accounts violating safety and legal standards will be terminated immediately.

While we maintain rigorous security and backups, Kolstø Development shall not be held liable for accidental service interruptions or localized hardware failures. We encourage users to export and back up digital PDF copies of their milestone book.
