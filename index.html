Security for your family's most cherished memories

Mainstream platforms monetize attention and sell user behavioral data. First Pages is engineered on an absolute premise: Parents retain complete, unconditional ownership of all uploaded content.

  • ✓ Zero Ad-Trackers: No advertising SDKs, cross-app profiling, or commercial data broking of any kind.
  • ✓ Unrestricted Ownership: Your photographs and written memories belong solely to you. Export your book or permanently delete your archive at any time.
  • ✓ Private Cloud Isolation: Photos are stored in private, access-controlled storage buckets protected by strict row-level security.
